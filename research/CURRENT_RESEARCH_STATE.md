## KEY MECHANISM: NS5 is TRIPLE-LOAD-BEARING (NEW — #985 closure 04:05 UTC May 24)

The cubic Newton-Schulz polynomial `(3/2)X − (1/2)X³` was previously characterized as a magnitude normalizer + rank-deficiency clipper. PR #985 (Shampoo body-Muon p=1/4 replacing NS5) catastrophic failure revealed a third structural role:

| Load-bearing role | Source | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **(1) Magnitude normalization** | #940 (post-NS Frob rescale catastrophic; pre-NS no-op) | NS5 contracts large-norm inputs to ‖polar‖_F ≈ √min(m,n) regardless of input scale |
| **(2) Rank-deficiency clipping** | #898 (`‖polar‖_F = √(m − rank(X))` identity) | NS5 caps Frob output at row/col-dim irrespective of input rank |
| **(3) Null-space amplification suppression** | **#985** | When NS5 removed and `matrix_neg_power(L_cov, 0.25, eps=1e-12)` applied to rank-1 L_cov at step 1, null eigenvalues clamp to 1e-12 then raise to negative power → 1000× amplification → 20,000× per-element RMS over baseline by step 25 |

**Design-space constraint for future body-Muon replacements:**
Any architecture that replaces NS5 must INDEPENDENTLY solve all three problems:
- Magnitude normalization (Frob-bound output)
- Rank-deficiency tolerance (output stable when input rank < dim)
- Null-space amplification suppression (no `eps^(-γ)` blow-up when γ > 0 and eps absolute)

**Implications:**
1. **Pure outer-product covariance preconditioners (Shampoo p=1/4) fail.** Confirmed structurally.
2. **Trace-relative eps clamps** (`eps = c·trace(M)/m`) directly attack problem (3) but don't solve (1) or (2). Worth testing as Shampoo refinement.
3. **PSGD-Kron Lie group preconditioners** (arxiv 2402.04553) solve (3) via Lie group invariance; (1) is preserved by Lie group constraint asymptotically; (2) needs Q_L/Q_R rank monitoring. Different fundamental approach.
4. **Hybrid layered preconditioners** that keep NS5 cubic as final stage (gets all three protections "for free") and modify upstream components: γ_power variants, alternative inverse-power approximations (Newton-Schulz for negative powers, Schur–Hermite, Padé). Lower-risk path.

This finding closes the "naive natural-gradient body-Muon" family and structurally pins NS5 cubic as load-bearing in three independent ways.
